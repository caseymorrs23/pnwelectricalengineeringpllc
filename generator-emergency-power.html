Common questions

Electrical engineering questions for this scope.

What generator projects are supported?

Support includes standby and emergency power design for commercial, multifamily, telecom, pump, life-safety support, and property infrastructure projects.

What deliverables are typical?

Typical deliverables include generator/ATS one-lines, load support, panel and feeder updates, standby distribution notes, equipment coordination, and permit drawings.

Can generator work be coordinated with service upgrades?

Yes. Generator and ATS work can be coordinated with service sizing, meter/main equipment, distribution changes, utility requirements, and AHJ permit review comments.

What items may be outside a basic permit package?

Fuel system design, acoustic studies, structural pads, detailed commissioning, field testing, site visits, or construction administration can be scoped separately where needed.
