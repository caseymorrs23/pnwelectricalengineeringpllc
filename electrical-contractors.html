Send enough information to avoid rework.

  • Mechanical/plumbing equipment schedules and cut sheets

  • Construction budget constraints or preferred equipment approach

Common questions

Electrical engineering questions for this scope.

What engineering support do electrical contractors usually need?

Most contractor-led projects need permit drawings, one-line diagrams, service/load calculations, panel schedules, equipment coordination, and stamped electrical plans when required by the AHJ.

Can PNW Electrical Engineering support design-build electrical bids?

Yes. The typical workflow is to review backgrounds and scope assumptions, identify electrical deliverables and exclusions, and provide a fixed-fee engineering proposal for the permit package.

What information should a contractor send first?

Helpful inputs include architectural PDFs, existing electrical drawings, utility information, mechanical and plumbing equipment schedules, project address, target permit date, and known owner requirements.

Which states are supported?

PNW Electrical Engineering provides licensed electrical engineering support in Washington, Oregon, Idaho, and Nevada, with scope limited to areas of demonstrated competency.
